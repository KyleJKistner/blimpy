# Envelope materials / converter request — DRAFT, NOT SENT

Please supply a sample quote for a helium-supported indoor robot, not hydrogen or heated flight. No production order is authorized. Nominal envelope: 62 cm equivalent-sphere diameter, 120–125 L actual volume. Optional equal-volume prolate sample: approximately 54.2 cm minor diameter × 81.2 cm length.

## Candidate materials

1. Torayfan PMX2, 15 µm, listed 73.3 m²/kg; request confirmation of complete finished basis weight and suitability as an unsupported, repeatedly handled helium envelope.
2. CEL-MET HS PET, approximately 12 µm/0.48 mil; confirm complete basis weight/yield including seal layer and metallization. The extracted sheet suggests 41,900 in²/lb, but confirm before committing the mass budget.
3. Capran PIR-MT1200, 12 µm/13.94 g/m², with sealing confined to seam strips. 3M 467MP is a coupon candidate, not an approved joining chemistry. A qualified seam-only heat-seal coating is also acceptable.
4. A conventional thin heat-sealable metallized nylon construction as the control sample.

Please quote small sheet/coupon samples first, plus conversion of three full envelopes only after coupon results. Give MOQ, minimum cutting width, converter contacts, lead time and shipping. Identify metal side, sealing side, full layer stack, coating mass, protective overcoats, storage/conditioning requirements and lot-to-lot tolerances. Do not substitute oxygen/moisture transmission for helium retention.

## Measurements requested

Conditioned complete dry mass including valve, neck, pole closures and load-spreading attachments; actual pressure-volume curve; helium permeation and complete-envelope loss; seam leak and creep after handling/flex cycles; allowable differential pressure; puncture/tear sensitivity; repair method; valve leakage and repeated-fill life.

Our 12-gore layout includes two 5 mm film flanges per seam and one adhesive interface, where used. It is a mass-estimating layout, not a validated flat pattern. Confirm the actual pattern and shape independently.

## Conditional full-envelope acceptance

Retain baseline maximum 36 g and at least 120 L actual cold volume. Stretch target is 21–26 g only if retention, handling and shape tests pass. At 20°C, 101325 Pa, 50% RH, ≥99.5% helium, target corrected net-lift loss ≤1.0 g/24 h over 72 h after thermal equilibration. Report environmental conditions and uncertainty, not just start/end uncorrected lift. A lighter but leaking film is not acceptable.

Please identify a safe low-pressure operating envelope and proof-test procedure before inflation; no high-temperature/overpressure test is authorized by this RFQ. A nominal 50 Pa calculation in our model is not a pressure rating.
