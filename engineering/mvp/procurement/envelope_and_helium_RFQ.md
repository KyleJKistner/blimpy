# Supplier questions — send before committing to a fleet
## Envelope
I am prototyping a supervised indoor helium robot. Please quote 3 samples and report:
1. Actual inflated external dimensions and enclosed volume in liters, not flat retail size.
   Target cold volume about 113L (~60cm true sphere); 120L+ if testing modest expansion.
2. Envelope + valve dry mass in grams; target <=32g. Payload mounts not included.
3. Film construction, recommended inflation pressure, rated temperature range, leak rate
   and expected helium retention over 24h. Is a sample compatible with 99%+ helium?
4. Can it be underfilled and expand roughly 7% in volume within its rated pressure?
   This is an experiment, not permission to heat a taut or unrated envelope.
5. Repairability, refill valve and price including shipping to San Diego.
I will select by measured net lift, not nominal size.

Example candidate for sampling only:
https://laballoons.com/products/24-inch-la-balloons-mirror-sphere-red-foil-balloon-r2512
No actual lift or film mass has been verified for this SKU.

regulator, pickup/delivery and return fees? What inflation valve and safe setup do you supply?
